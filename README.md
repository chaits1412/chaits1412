## Featured Work

### [Data on Demand — Quick-Commerce Gap & Customer Experience Analysis](https://github.com/chaits1412/Quick-commerce-analytics)

A diagnostic analytics project on a simulated Zepto/Blinkit-style quick-commerce business — 150,000 searches and 78,936 orders — asking where the operational gaps sit between what customers want and what the business delivers.

**Python → BigQuery SQL → Root-Cause Analysis → Power BI → Recommendations**

- Found a **54.28% product availability gap** across customer searches, concentrated in identifiable SKUs
- Investigated a single underperforming dark store (DS009: 6.13% on-time rate vs. a ~42% network average) across four candidate causes — peak-hour load, delivery distance, order workload, and picking time — and ruled out all four, flagging it as an unexplained anomaly rather than forcing a conclusion
- 26 documented BigQuery queries, each with its business purpose and result recorded inline
- 3-page interactive Power BI diagnostic dashboard

`Python` `BigQuery` `SQL` `Power BI` `DAX`
